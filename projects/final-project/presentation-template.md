# Final Project — Presentation Template

**Length:** 6 minutes + 2–3 minutes Q&A · **Slides:** 6 · Sessions W16 S31–32

Use the timing as a hard budget: Slide 1 (0:30) · Slide 2 (1:00) ·
Slide 3 (1:30) · Slide 4 (2:00) · Slide 5 (1:00) · Demo (optional, within
slide 4's budget).

---

## Slide 1 — Question (30 sec)

- One sentence: the question.
- One sentence: why it matters (audience + decision from the proposal).
- **Pitfall to avoid:** two questions, or a question the data can't
  answer.

## Slide 2 — Data (1 min)

- Source, license, shape (rows × columns).
- ONE quality issue you found and fixed (proves the cleaning skill).
- **Pitfall to avoid:** no license, or a wall of columns.

## Slide 3 — Method (1.5 min)

- Model(s) + metric.
- One line on *why this method* (not just what it is).
- Baseline comparison (one number: model X vs. baseline Y).
- **Pitfall to avoid:** jargon without meaning; no baseline.

## Slide 4 — Results (2 min)

- ONE chart + the ONE number that answers the question.
- One sentence of interpretation tied to the decision in Slide 1.
- Optional 30-sec live demo of the Streamlit app (have a fallback:
  screenshot or printed output if the demo fails).
- **Pitfall to avoid:** five charts; a number with no context.

## Slide 5 — Limits & next steps (1 min)

- One data limitation, one modeling limitation.
- One concrete next step if given another week.
- **Pitfall to avoid:** "our model is perfect."

## Slide 6 — Thank you + takeaway (30 sec)

- One-line takeaway: "<dataset> shows <finding>, so <decision>."
- Invite questions.

---

## Demo checklist (live or fallback)

- [ ] `streamlit run app.py` verified the day before, data cached.
- [ ] Fallback ready: saved screenshot + printed headline numbers.
- [ ] One scripted interaction: "I'll select Islamabad, and watch the
  line plot update."

## Q&A prep (answer in ≤ 30 seconds each)

- Why did you choose this dataset/model/metric?
- What would you do with more data/time?
- What could go wrong with your conclusion?
- How do you know your results are reproducible?
- Which part did each teammate own?

## Rehearsal rules

- Hard stop at 6:00 — rehearse with a timer twice.
- Slides must be readable at arm's length (no paragraphs).
- The audience is non-technical managers: define every metric you name.
