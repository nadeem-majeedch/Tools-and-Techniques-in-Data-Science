End-to-end data science project in teams of 2–3, integrating all three CLOs.

## Requirements (summary)

1. **Question & data (CLO-1):** a clear, answerable question and a documented,
   openly licensed dataset (from `../datasets/` or an approved source).
2. **Cleaning & EDA (CLO-1):** reproducible cleaning and exploration with Pandas
   and visualizations.
3. **Modeling (CLO-2):** at least one basic scikit-learn model with an honest
   evaluation (train/test split, appropriate metrics).
4. **AI-assisted component (CLO-3):** at least one of — PandasAI/Ollama-assisted
   analysis step, or an n8n automation for data collection/processing.
5. **Reproducibility & ethics reflection (CLO-3):** environment + seed
   documentation and a short reflection on bias, privacy, provenance, and how AI
   assistance was used (per the AI-use policy).

## Deliverables

- Proposal: one page (question, data source, planned approach).
- GitHub repository: organized project (see `resources/setup-guide.md` for
  recommended project layout), executed notebooks, `requirements.txt`.
- Presentation: 10 minutes + 3 minutes Q&A.
- Final report/reflection: ~1,000 words including the ethics and reproducibility
  reflection.
