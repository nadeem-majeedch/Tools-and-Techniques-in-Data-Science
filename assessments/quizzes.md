# Quizzes

Two short quizzes check your understanding of each half of the course before
the bigger exams. They are **closed-notes, in-class, ~25 minutes**, given at
the start of the session shown below. Full details and policies are in the
[Assessment Plan](../assessment-plan.md).

| Quiz | When | Covers (weeks) | CLOs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quiz 1** | Week 8 · Session 15 (start of class) | Weeks 1–7 — Python, Jupyter, Git/GitHub, NumPy, Pandas, data cleaning, data acquisition & combining, visualization | CLO-1 |
| **Quiz 2** | Week 15 · Session 29 (start of class) | Weeks 8–14 — EDA, intro to ML, regression, classification, clustering, pipelines & cross-validation, reproducibility, LLMs, PandasAI, Ollama, tool calling & agents, Streamlit | CLO-2, CLO-3 |

See the [Course Schedule](../weekly-schedule.md) for all key dates.

## Question types

Questions test **understanding and application, not memorization**. You will
see a mix of:

- **Multiple choice** with plausible distractors
- **Code tracing** — "what does this code print?"
- **Debugging** — find the bug in a short, realistic snippet
- **Short answer** — explain a concept in 1–3 sentences (e.g., *why* fill a
  missing value with the group median rather than dropping the row)
- **Conceptual / scenario** items that ask you to apply several skills to a
  small situation

??? info "Quiz questions reuse course examples with variations"
    Quizzes are drawn from the same ideas and datasets you have already seen in
    the sessions, labs, and notebooks — but with the numbers, columns, or
    context changed. If you understood the example, you can answer the
    variation.

## How to prepare

1. **After every session**, answer that session's *"Short assessment
   questions"* section out loud or in writing — they are quiz-format practice.
2. **Do every lab.** Quiz items mirror lab checkpoint questions.
3. Review the session summaries — each lecture page lists its key concepts and
   terminology.
4. For Quiz 1, [Lab 16 · Midterm Review](../labs/lab-16-midterm-review.md)
   gives a timed practice run in the same format.
5. Re-run the [course notebooks](../course-notebooks/README.md) from Module A
   (Quiz 1) or Modules B–C (Quiz 2) so the code is in your fingers, not just
   your eyes.

## Policies to remember

- Closed notes; no saved notebooks; **no AI assistants** (the course AI-use
  policy in the [Assessment Plan](../assessment-plan.md) applies).
- Quizzes are graded in class right after submission — expect a quick review
  discussion of the common mistakes.
- Answer keys are distributed by your instructor and are **not published** on
  this site.
